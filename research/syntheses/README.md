# Syntheses Research

This directory stores cross-cutting synthesis documents derived from concept, capability, and product notes.

## What Belongs Here
- vocabulary and boundary definitions
- comparison matrices
- repository-level policies
- cross-topic synthesis notes
- distilled principles
- shared cross-note record models

## What Does Not Belong Here
- raw concept notes that stand on their own
- capability-specific notes
- product snapshots

## Purpose
These files act as durable glue across the rest of the research tree.
